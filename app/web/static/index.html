Batch

One CSV row becomes one job. Columns: image,video,character,scene,seeds,passes,note and optionally engine (wan, scail, seedance). image and video are file names already in the pod's ComfyUI/input folder (upload them through Jupyter on port 8888). Character and scene are preset names. passes is 1 to 3 and is capped by the video length.

Costs

Check the real numbers on RunPod under Billing and on comfy.org under Credits. The pod estimate is a floor, not an invoice.

How to get a good clip

Reference image

Full body head to toe, facing the camera, face clearly visible, sharp, 1024 to 2048 px on the long side, plain background. The face comes from this image.

Driving video

One person, full body, stable camera, even light, no motion blur. Each pass uses 81 frames (about 3.4 s at 24 fps); a longer video allows 2 or 3 passes for a longer clip. The motion comes from this video.

Engines

Wan Animate 2 generates a new background from the scene text. SCAIL-2 swaps the character into the original video and keeps its background, lighting and camera; describe the whole scene in the text (Describe from the driving video does this) and expect the frame to follow the video's orientation. Seedance 2.5 is ByteDance's paid model run through ComfyUI: no GPU, the whole clip (4 to 30 s) with sound at 480p, 720p or 1080p by tier, billed in Comfy credits.

Seeds and tiers

Two to four seeds give you choices; each clip shows a contact sheet and a face strip so you can pick in seconds. Fast is a preview. Standard is what we scored 23 of 25. High uses the full model at 720p and takes longer.

Finish

A second pass that upscales 2× (about 1080p), doubles the frame rate, adds a light camera grain and keeps the driving video's sound. A few minutes per seed.

Rules

Only characters we invented or people who signed consent. Label AI content. Vary content types. Never share this link outside the team.
