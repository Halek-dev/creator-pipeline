# Fix the reference before touching the sampler

The best SCAIL-2 replacement our pipeline can produce comes from fixing the inputs, not from adding steps. Three of the four flaws in the 17 October runs trace to the reference image and to how identities are read from it: the figurines became extra people because SAM3 tracked them as "human" and SCAIL-2 binds every coloured identity to a slot, the grey rim came from a cut-out the model never needed (the Comfy node already composites the reference onto black in replacement mode, so our grey version only changed what CLIP Vision saw), and the plastic jeans are what any model invents when the reference stops at the hips and the driver shows legs. The fourth flaw, the driving person's clothing showing through, is the failure the SCAIL-2 maintainers attribute to the LightX2V **I2V** LoRA that the official template ships; they recommend the Wan 2.1 **T2V** LightX2V LoRA in three separate issues ([Issue #21](https://github.com/zai-org/SCAIL-2/issues/21), [Issue #25](https://github.com/zai-org/SCAIL-2/issues/25), [Issue #30](https://github.com/zai-org/SCAIL-2/issues/30)). The authors' own recipe is explicit and cheap to follow: reference cut out onto **black** with the SAM mask, posed, scaled and framed like the first driving frame at the output resolution (**704x1280 preferred, 512x896 acceptable**, both divisible by 32), only the intended person coloured, a long descriptive prompt, and 81-frame segments with a 5-frame overlap ([README](https://github.com/zai-org/SCAIL-2), [model card](https://huggingface.co/zai-org/SCAIL-2/blob/main/README.md)). Our Standard tier is already a value-for-value copy of the template's turbo path (6 steps, cfg 1, shift 5, euler/simple, DPO 1.0, distill 0.8), so it is as official as a preset gets; our High tier at 16 steps and cfg 3.5 matches nothing published, since the maintainers' non-distilled default is **40 to 50 steps, cfg 4, shift 5** ([Issue #29](https://github.com/zai-org/SCAIL-2/issues/29)) and the template's quality toggle is 40 steps, cfg 5, shift 5. No intermediate preset exists anywhere; the maintainers' own direction for more detail (lower the distill strength, raise the steps from 4 to 8) is the defensible basis for one, at about 1.7x the Standard render time instead of the roughly 13x a 40-step cfg-4 pass costs. The change list below therefore puts reference and identity handling first (author-backed, free), the T2V LoRA second, tiers and resolution third, and prompt and finishing last, with a test protocol that isolates each change on one 81-frame segment of the same pair at about 10 minutes and $0.10 each on an A40.

## Three of the four flaws are input problems, not sampler problems

The mechanism matters because it decides where to spend effort. SCAIL-2 does not inpaint. The paper describes the backbone receiving the reference latent, the driving-video latent and the noisy latent as one in-context sequence, with an "Environment Switch" mask channel telling the model whether the scene comes from the reference (animation) or from the driving video (replacement) and six coloured "Binding Slot" channels tying each reference character to a tracked person; the environment mask is simply "the complement of the union of either the driving or the reference character masks" ([arXiv 2606.10804](https://arxiv.org/html/2606.10804)). Two consequences follow directly. First, **anything SAM3 colours becomes a bound subject**, which is exactly how two shelf figurines were composited into the clip as extra people ([TEST_LOG.md](C:/Users/user/Desktop/creator-pipeline/docs/TEST_LOG.md)). Second, the background is regenerated under a strong "keep the driving environment" signal rather than copied, so where the driver's silhouette was wider than our character the model has to invent background, and that is where the driver's clothing can bleed through; the maintainers' explanation for the same symptom in [Issue #21](https://github.com/zai-org/SCAIL-2/issues/21) is "the incompatibility of LightX2V loras with our model" rather than a mask fault.

The grey rim has a specific and avoidable cause. Inside `WanSCAILToVideo`, in replacement mode, each reference is already multiplied by its coloured mask (any pixel whose mask channel exceeds 0.1 is kept, everything else becomes black) before the VAE encode ([nodes_scail.py](file:///C:/Users/user/Desktop/creator-pipeline/ComfyUI/comfy_extras/nodes_scail.py); [WanSCAILToVideo docs](file:///C:/Users/user/Desktop/creator-pipeline/.venv/Lib/site-packages/comfyui_embedded_docs/docs/WanSCAILToVideo/en.md)). The authors' black-background requirement ([Issue #7](https://github.com/zai-org/SCAIL-2/issues/7), [Issue #12](https://github.com/zai-org/SCAIL-2/issues/12)) is therefore met by the Comfy graph for the VAE path as long as the mask covers the right person. Our `clean_reference` chain composited the photo onto **0x808080 grey** after a 6 px `GrowMask`, and a `FeatherMask` with left/top/right/bottom set to 6, which feathers the canvas border rather than the object contour ([nodes_mask.py](file:///C:/Users/user/Desktop/creator-pipeline/ComfyUI/comfy_extras/nodes_mask.py)), so the visible edge was a hard grey boundary 6 px outside the hair ([build_scail.py](C:/Users/user/Desktop/creator-pipeline/tools/build_scail.py)). That image then fed both the node, which re-masked it, and `CLIPVisionEncode` with crop "none", which did not. The only thing the grey cut-out changed in the model's view was the CLIP Vision embedding, and the rim appeared with it. Grey is also not a documented value anywhere: the README's mask semantics are black for "background not visible" and white for "visible" ([README](https://github.com/zai-org/SCAIL-2)), and the one maintainer remark about CLIP Vision is a warning to "check whether the CLIP vision model handles black-background references well, since that is a common workflow flaw" ([Issue #30](https://github.com/zai-org/SCAIL-2/issues/30)), which is ambiguous enough that raw versus black-cut-out into CLIP Vision has to be an A/B test rather than a rule. Nothing official mentions halos or hair edges at all.

The jeans are a framing problem. The model regenerates the whole figure from the driving motion, so unseen body parts are invented; the paper does not address this and the authors offer no direct guidance, only the alignment advice and the experimental multi-reference input ([arXiv 2606.10804](https://arxiv.org/html/2606.10804), [README](https://github.com/zai-org/SCAIL-2)). The maintainers do say that "SCAIL-2 leans toward the reference image's framing" and "tends to refer more to the initial position of reference image than the driving video", producing oversized characters from tightly framed photos and "teleporting" at segment starts ([Issue #21](https://github.com/zai-org/SCAIL-2/issues/21), [Issue #25](https://github.com/zai-org/SCAIL-2/issues/25)). A waist-up reference on a full-body dance clip is the worst case for both effects at once. "Plastic" texture on the invented region compounds it: smooth skin is "a common trait of the base model", and the authors' levers are higher resolution and a textured close-up as a second reference ([Issue #20](https://github.com/zai-org/SCAIL-2/issues/20)), while the only lever anyone has documented for the missing body is outpainting the reference to full length and to the video's aspect before the run ([note.com](https://note.com/mattariyukkuri/n/n5e8b4452a120?hl=en)).

## Black cut-out, matched framing, full body: the reference rules

The reference carries more of the result than any sampler setting, and the authors say so repeatedly. In replacement mode "the model expects the reference image to have a black background", the official preprocessing scripts do not do it automatically, and applying the SAM mask with everything outside the subject set to black fixed two separate bug reports where the output inherited the reference's room instead of the video's ([Issue #7](https://github.com/zai-org/SCAIL-2/issues/7), [Issue #12](https://github.com/zai-org/SCAIL-2/issues/12), [Issue #30](https://github.com/zai-org/SCAIL-2/issues/30)). The pose videos used in training also had black backgrounds, which is why the maintainers prefer black over white when users mask the driving video in animation mode ([Issue #17](https://github.com/zai-org/SCAIL-2/issues/17)). Community workflows follow suit: the Civitai replacement workflow states "Character replacement uses character cutout images with black backgrounds" ([Civitai 2716835](https://civitai.com/models/2716835/scail2-motion-transfer-character-replacement-workflow)). For our graph the practical reading is that the node already does the black composite for the latent, so a pre-composite is only worth keeping if it helps the CLIP Vision path; if it stays, it must be black, use the hard SAM mask with no grow and no feather, and be filtered to the same identity index as the colored mask.

Framing is the second rule and the one with the most leverage on scale and position. The maintainers recommend "aligning the reference image with the first frame of the driving video" in pose, scale and camera position, and note that "most workflows resize and crop driving video and reference image to a unified resolution", giving 704x1280 and 512x896 as the examples ([Issue #25](https://github.com/zai-org/SCAIL-2/issues/25)). The Wan2GP tester with published example videos found that a mismatched aspect made the swapped figure "look squashed" with weaker resemblance, a mismatched opening pose made the start "look melted", front-facing references beat profile and back views, and outpainting the reference to the video's aspect was the fix ([note.com](https://note.com/mattariyukkuri/n/n5e8b4452a120?hl=en)). This matters for our graph because neither we nor the template pre-frame the reference: the node bicubic-resizes it to the output size with a **center crop**, so a square or landscape photo fed into 512x896 silently loses its sides, and a 9:16 photo fed into the template's 896x512 loses top and bottom ([nodes_scail.py](file:///C:/Users/user/Desktop/creator-pipeline/ComfyUI/comfy_extras/nodes_scail.py); [template](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Comfy-Org/workflow_templates/main/templates/video_wan21_scail2_character_replacement_int8.json)). Padding to the output aspect, never cropping, is the rule, and the padding colour should be black for the same reason as the cut-out. The one documented good run used a 768x1152 full-body reference into 896x512 output ([comfylab.dev](https://comfylab.dev/blog/workflows/scail-2-character-replacement-comfyui-workflow/)); nobody has published a minimum reference resolution.

Full-body references for full-body videos are our own rule from the test log, and the research supports it by inference rather than by author statement. No author text recommends full-body framing, but every mechanism points the same way: the character is anchored to the reference framing, the model invents what it cannot see, and the original SCAIL training data was filtered so poses were "visually complete", covering "either the upper body or the full body" ([arXiv 2512.05905](https://arxiv.org/html/2512.05905v1), the earlier pose-based model, not SCAIL-2). For the user's waist-up photo the two documented options are to outpaint it to full length with an image-edit model, as the Wan2GP author did for aspect and as the first-frame-edit recipe does for composition ([stablediffusiontutorials](https://www.stablediffusiontutorials.com/2026/06/wan2.1-scail2.html)), or to pick a different photo. Describing the lower-body clothing in the prompt is the cheap complement, since the README asks for "the replacement character's visible clothing and appearance" in replacement prompts ([README](https://github.com/zai-org/SCAIL-2)).

Additional views are available but not a first resort. The Comfy node accepts extra batch images as "additional views (back view, close-up, occluded background), each needing a matching reference_image_mask in that identity's color" ([nodes_scail.py](file:///C:/Users/user/Desktop/creator-pipeline/ComfyUI/comfy_extras/nodes_scail.py)), the authors ship the equivalent flags, and they suggested "a close-up portrait with clear skin texture as a second reference" for smooth skin ([Issue #20](https://github.com/zai-org/SCAIL-2/issues/20)). The same authors call multi-reference "not optimized for such inputs" with video quality that "may degrade even though additional information do get referenced" ([README](https://github.com/zai-org/SCAIL-2)), and the scail-auto-extend author reports that it "binds by color but blends appearances" ([scail-auto-extend](https://github.com/Brobert-in-aus/scail-auto-extend)). For a single-person swap the consensus is one well-framed image plus a detailed prompt; a second close-up reference is a texture experiment to run after the framing fix, not before.

## Index 0 by area stopped the figurines but will drop a second dancer

The official template leaves identity selection wide open: SAM3 text "human" on the reference, `detection_threshold` 0.5, `max_objects` 4, `object_indices` "" (all objects) and `sort_by` "left_to_right" on both tracks ([template](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Comfy-Org/workflow_templates/main/templates/video_wan21_scail2_character_replacement_int8.json)). With those values any object SAM3 is willing to call "human" in the reference, including figurines, posters or a reflection, gets a colour and a slot, and the leftmost one is paired with the leftmost driving person. Our fix in commit f567caa, `object_indices "0"` with `sort_by "area"`, uses the official mechanism: the node documents `object_indices` as "person indices to include ... Applied to both reference and pose video masks" and `area` as "biggest object (by mask area at first appearance) gets the first color" ([SCAIL2ColoredMask docs](file:///C:/Users/user/Desktop/creator-pipeline/.venv/Lib/site-packages/comfyui_embedded_docs/docs/SCAIL2ColoredMask/en.md)). Because the filter happens before the node's black composite, it also closes the VAE leak path; this is the right default for single-person clips and the one change from the 17 October session that has not yet been verified live.

The trade-off is multi-person clips. Our filter keeps exactly one identity in the driving video as well, so a second real dancer is demoted to environment and regenerated as scenery rather than kept and recoloured as identity 1, which is what the template would do. Two independent node authors also warn that `area` ordering "can change identity order when one person becomes larger" and that `left_to_right` is "usually the most predictable" for two-person shots ([ComfyUI-SCAIL-Pose2](https://github.com/rookiestar28/ComfyUI-SCAIL-Pose2), [scail-auto-extend](https://github.com/Brobert-in-aus/scail-auto-extend)), although both sort keys are evaluated at first appearance, so the risk is in which person is largest or leftmost in frame one, not mid-clip. For two-person clips the author guidance is colour consistency: the person to replace and the reference person "need the same color", characters entering mid-shot "may cause tracking or coloring errors", and multi-reference binding is "a zero-shot function, so the above may not always work" ([Issue #25](https://github.com/zai-org/SCAIL-2/issues/25), [Issue #32](https://github.com/zai-org/SCAIL-2/issues/32)). The practical policy is therefore a per-clip choice exposed in the app: single person, keep index 0 by area; two people, switch to `left_to_right` and list the indices explicitly, split the clip where someone enters or leaves, and preview the coloured masks before spending render time, which is what the template's two disabled `PreviewImage` nodes were for.

The SAM text prompt is the template's own tool for cluttered scenes and we are using it inconsistently. The template note says "use the same word when subject matches; use different words if video/ref need different focus (e.g. crowded scene, be more specific)", and the Int8 sample uses "girl, handkerchief" on the driving track to capture a prop ([template](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Comfy-Org/workflow_templates/main/templates/video_wan21_scail2_character_replacement_int8.json)); our graph uses "person" on the video and "human" on the reference for the same subject ([build_scail.py](C:/Users/user/Desktop/creator-pipeline/tools/build_scail.py)). A more specific noun such as "woman" on both tracks is the template-sanctioned way to stop SAM3 proposing figurines in the first place, and the Wan2GP tester's single-person setting was a specific keyword with max objects 1, with the comment that "a bad mask leads to messy output, so the mask step matters most" ([note.com](https://note.com/mattariyukkuri/n/n5e8b4452a120?hl=en)). Lowering `max_objects` to 1 on the reference tracker is tempting but riskier than the index filter, because SAM3's internal order is not documented as "largest first"; the index-0-by-area filter after a 4-object track is the safer single-person guard.

## The I2V LightX2V LoRA is the maintainers' prime suspect

The template ships `lightx2v_I2V_14B_480p_cfg_step_distill_rank64_bf16.safetensors` at strength 0.8 from Kijai's repository, and our graph copies it ([template](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Comfy-Org/workflow_templates/main/templates/video_wan21_scail2_character_replacement_int8.json), [build_scail.py](C:/Users/user/Desktop/creator-pipeline/tools/build_scail.py)). The maintainers disagree with that choice in writing. On the clothing-transfer report, where a yellow tutu from the driving dancer faded in and out on the swapped character despite prompts, negative prompts, LoRA rank changes and mask variations, the maintainer wrote "we attribute this to the incompatibility of LightX2V loras with our model" and "my primary recommendation is setting the LightX2V lora to a T2V one" ([Issue #21](https://github.com/zai-org/SCAIL-2/issues/21)); the same recommendation appears in the replacement-instability recipe of [Issue #25](https://github.com/zai-org/SCAIL-2/issues/25) and in the lighting thread, "use a lightx2v t2v lora instead of an i2v one if you're using the relight lora" ([Issue #30](https://github.com/zai-org/SCAIL-2/issues/30)). The README's own distilled example uses `--lora_alpha 1.0` with 8 steps, shift 1 and guidance 1.0 ([README](https://github.com/zai-org/SCAIL-2)). The research did not retrieve the exact T2V file name; the I2V file comes from `Kijai/WanVideo_comfy`, which is where the rank-64 T2V sibling should be confirmed before the pod session. Honesty requires noting that the Issue #21 reporter tried "some T2V LoRAs" without a clean result and the issue is still open, so the T2V swap is the highest-probability fix for clothing leak, not a guaranteed one.

The maintainers also supply the detail knob for distilled runs. Asked about texture, they said to lower the LightX2V strength or raise the steps, giving 4 to 8 as the example range ([Issue #29](https://github.com/zai-org/SCAIL-2/issues/29)), and a community alternative in the same thread ran a FusionX LoRA at 0.8 to 1.0 with 8 to 12 steps at shift 3 ([Issue #29](https://github.com/zai-org/SCAIL-2/issues/29)). The DPO LoRA stays on in every configuration: it targets hand articulation and improves lip and eye sync ([README](https://github.com/zai-org/SCAIL-2), [arXiv 2606.10804](https://arxiv.org/html/2606.10804)), with the single caveat that if the face drifts toward the driver's, the authors list "reducing the strength of DPO lora" alongside a more detailed prompt and multiple references ([Issue #27](https://github.com/zai-org/SCAIL-2/issues/27)); no author gives a number, and the template's 1.0 is the only published value.

A fallback mode exists if replacement keeps misbehaving. The maintainers describe a recipe they say "tends to be more stable in some cases than directly relying on replacement mode": reference on black posed to match the first frame, T2V LightX2V, animation mode with replacement off, optional relight LoRA ([Issue #25](https://github.com/zai-org/SCAIL-2/issues/25)), and they note community members "often find it better than replacement mode" ([Issue #21](https://github.com/zai-org/SCAIL-2/issues/21)). In the Comfy graph this is `replace=False` on both `SCAIL2ColoredMask` and `WanSCAILToVideo` with a rewritten prompt, since in animation mode the environment comes from the reference and the driving mask background flips to black ([docs.comfy.org](https://docs.comfy.org/tutorials/video/zai/scail2)). It will not keep the video's studio, so for our use case it is a diagnostic and a last resort, not a default.

## Presets stop at 6/1/5 and 40 to 50/4/5 with nothing between

Three author-adjacent presets exist and they do not fully agree. The template's turbo path, which produced Comfy's example output, is **euler, simple, 6 steps, cfg 1.0, shift 5**, DPO 1.0, distill 0.8, seed 1 fixed, decoding the `denoised_output`; its one-toggle quality alternative is **40 steps, cfg 5.0, shift 5** with the distill LoRA switched out of the model chain ([template](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Comfy-Org/workflow_templates/main/templates/video_wan21_scail2_character_replacement_int8.json)). The upstream CLI defaults are 40 steps, guidance 5.0, shift 3.0, UniPC ([README](https://github.com/zai-org/SCAIL-2)). The maintainers' later, experience-based statement is "our default settings without LightX2V are Shift: 5, Steps: 40 - 50, and CFG: 4", with the remark that cfg 4 beat cfg 5 in their experiments ([Issue #29](https://github.com/zai-org/SCAIL-2/issues/29)). Shift 5 is common to the template and the maintainers, so it is settled; cfg 4 over 5 is the maintainers' call and should be ours. Our High tier, 16 steps at cfg 3.5 without distill ([build_scail.py](C:/Users/user/Desktop/creator-pipeline/tools/build_scail.py)), sits in no published preset, and no community source publishes a sampler or shift sweep for SCAIL-2 in ComfyUI.

The cost of the real High preset decides how it should be used. ComfyUI runs one model pass per step at cfg 1.0 and two per step when guidance is on, so the template turbo path is 6 passes, our current High is 32 passes (about 5x, which is where `SCAIL_HIGH_FACTOR = 5` in [jobs.py](C:/Users/user/Desktop/creator-pipeline/app/jobs.py) comes from), and the maintainers' 40 steps at cfg 4 is 80 passes, roughly **13x the Standard sampling time**. The project's only cost anchor for SCAIL-2 is the published 571 s per 81-frame 896x512 segment on an RTX 3090 with int8, which the running-costs report budgets at about **$0.10 per segment and $0.29 per 10 s clip on an A40**, roughly double at 704p ([Motion swap engine running costs](C:/Users/user/Desktop/creator-pipeline/reports/Motion%20swap%20engine%20running%20costs.md), [comfylab.dev](https://comfylab.dev/blog/workflows/scail-2-character-replacement-comfyui-workflow/)); the live 3-segment Standard run on the RTX PRO 4500 took about 20 minutes and $0.25 ([TEST_LOG.md](C:/Users/user/Desktop/creator-pipeline/docs/TEST_LOG.md)). Scaling the sampling share of that time by 13 puts a single 40-step cfg-4 segment at roughly 1.5 to 2 hours and $1 or more on an A40 at 512p, and several hours at 704p; these are arithmetic extrapolations, not measurements, but they are enough to say that the author-grade High tier is a final-render-only setting, and that the tier the team will actually use for iteration is the one nobody has published.

Resolution is the other half of the quality budget, and here the authors are unambiguous: "pose-driven and replacement performs better at 704p", 512p and 704p are both supported, and height and width must each be divisible by 32 ([model card](https://huggingface.co/zai-org/SCAIL-2/blob/main/README.md)); training ran from 512x896 to 704x1280 on 65- or 81-frame clips ([arXiv 2606.10804](https://arxiv.org/html/2606.10804)). The template runs the lower tier, 896x512, and the docs' "divisible by 16" line conflicts with the node's step of 32 and the template's own `(a // 32) * 32` math; our `snap32` is correct, and the scail-auto-extend author explains why it matters, since non-multiples of 32 are circular-padded and wrap the top edge onto the bottom ([scail-auto-extend](https://github.com/Brobert-in-aus/scail-auto-extend)). The only published 704p-class comparison is the Wan2GP tester's: 720x1280 took about 3x as long as 480x832 and was "noticeably better quality" ([note.com](https://note.com/mattariyukkuri/n/n5e8b4452a120?hl=en)). VRAM at 704p is unverified anywhere; the one measured run put the int8 model at 15.9 GB plus 6.4 GB text encoder and 1.2 GB CLIP Vision, peaking under 24 GB at 896x512 ([comfylab.dev](https://comfylab.dev/blog/workflows/scail-2-character-replacement-comfyui-workflow/)), so a 48 GB A40 is the sensible card for the 704p test and the 32 GB RTX PRO 4500 should be treated as unproven for it. For our portrait clips the 704p frame is 704x1280.

## Prompt, chaining and finishing are second-order but not optional

The prompt is a real lever with a clear author position and one contradicting data point. The README says prompts should describe the generated video rather than instruct, that SCAIL-2 "is trained on long, detailed prompts", that short or empty prompts run but detailed subject and motion descriptions usually do better, and that replacement prompts should "describe the video after replacement" including the new character's visible clothing and nearby objects it interacts with ([README](https://github.com/zai-org/SCAIL-2)); a more detailed prompt is also one of the three author mitigations for identity leak ([Issue #27](https://github.com/zai-org/SCAIL-2/issues/27)). The templates practise this: the Int8 sample prompt is a roughly 90-word paragraph covering subject, clothing, action, foreground, background and lighting, and the fp16 sample adds explicit artifact avoidance, "She has a normal-sized head and a slim face, with no hat, no headwear, and no oversized hair volume" ([template fp16](https://github.com/Comfy-Org/workflow_templates/blob/main/templates/video_wan21_scail2_character_replacement.json)). Negative prompts are empty in both templates and were useless against the Issue #21 clothing leak, so they stay empty. The contradiction is a reporter in [Issue #25](https://github.com/zai-org/SCAIL-2/issues/25) who found "a very short prompt that didn't mention the reference person worked" where a long one failed, unanswered by the maintainers; treat prompt length as the first thing to vary when a clip misfires, not as a rule. Our builder's default is a one-sentence placeholder, and the app passes the job's character prompt, which is assembled in the "Character appearance description ... Background description" format used for Seedance ([jobs.py](C:/Users/user/Desktop/creator-pipeline/app/jobs.py)); SCAIL-2 should get a composed paragraph that names the lower-body garment explicitly.

Segment chaining is the one area where our graph and the template are identical and the authors agree with both. The node tooltip states "SCAIL-2 trained at 5 (81-frame chunks, 76-frame step)", the template slices the driving video at 76-frame offsets, drops the 5 overlap frames of each Extend segment and colour-matches the remainder to the previous segment's last frame with `ColorTransfer` in `reinhard_lab`, `per_frame`, strength 1.0 ([template](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Comfy-Org/workflow_templates/main/templates/video_wan21_scail2_character_replacement_int8.json), [nodes_scail.py](file:///C:/Users/user/Desktop/creator-pipeline/ComfyUI/comfy_extras/nodes_scail.py)), and our seg2 graph does the same ([our_graph_vs_template](C:/Users/user/Desktop/creator-pipeline/research_notes/SCAIL%202%20best%20practice%20settings/our_graph_vs_template.md)). The known weakness is cumulative: the maintainers acknowledge colour shift from loop sampling and teleporting at context-window boundaries and say "the team is still working on both" ([Issue #21](https://github.com/zai-org/SCAIL-2/issues/21)), and the Wan2GP thread documents desaturation and invented accessories over long replacement runs that improved "markedly ... though not eliminated" when the reference was injected as a short still video ([Wan2GP #2098](https://github.com/deepbeepmeep/Wan2GP/issues/2098)). That trick is not in Comfy core, so for the app the mitigations are the ones we already have plus keeping clips at three segments, which the running-costs report also favours. Neither graph colour-matches the character to the driving video, only segment to segment; the authors' answer to lighting mismatch is the relight LoRA (`wan2.1_SCAIL_2_relight_lora_bf16.safetensors` in the Comfy-Org repo, replacement mode only, to be used with the T2V LightX2V), which they themselves call imperfect and which altered the character at strength 3 in the one detailed report ([Issue #30](https://github.com/zai-org/SCAIL-2/issues/30), [HF Comfy-Org/SCAIL-2](https://huggingface.co/Comfy-Org/SCAIL-2)); it is an experiment for the end of the list, started at a low strength as RunComfy advises ([RunComfy](https://www.runcomfy.com/comfyui-workflows/scail-2-motion-transfer-in-comfyui-reference-image-to-video)).

Post-processing is where the template does nothing and the community does a lot. The template decodes, drops overlaps, colour-matches and saves at the source fps with the source audio; no upscale, interpolation, sharpening or mask work ([template](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Comfy-Org/workflow_templates/main/templates/video_wan21_scail2_character_replacement_int8.json)). Community finishing stacks add a second higher-resolution sampling pass, NVIDIA VSR and RIFE interpolation ([Civitai 2704602](https://civitai.com/models/2704602/scail2-long-videos-high-res-fix-nvidia-vsr-interpolation)), FILM interpolation after generating at 16 fps ([scail-auto-extend](https://github.com/Brobert-in-aus/scail-auto-extend), a single author's fps claim that the paper's "mixed resolutions and fps" statement neither confirms nor denies), and a face-crop refinement pass that crops, refines and composites the face back ([comfyui_scail2_multi_cond](https://github.com/TTPlanetPig/comfyui_scail2_multi_cond)). Our optional Finish pass (SeedVR2 upscale plus FILM interpolation) is the same category and the costs report prices it at roughly a pass-length per chunk; it improves resolution and smoothness, not the plastic texture or the invented jeans, which have to be fixed upstream. The pose knobs the app already exposes deserve one caution: our "motion looseness" drives `pose_end`, and the only report of lowering `pose_end` and `pose_strength` curing "rigid and rubbery" motion is from a user, with the maintainer suspecting a LoRA or segmentation issue instead ([Issue #18](https://github.com/zai-org/SCAIL-2/issues/18)); keep it at 1.0 for the test protocol so it does not confound the other changes.

## Our graph is the template's turbo path plus three deliberate changes

A node-by-node comparison against the installed Int8 template shows that at Standard tier every sampler, LoRA, mask-threshold, segment and colour-transfer value is identical to the template's turbo path, including the decode from `denoised_output`, seed 1, `pose_strength` 1.0, `pose_start` 0, `pose_end` 1, `previous_frame_count` 5 and the `(a // 32) * 32` snap ([our_graph_vs_template](C:/Users/user/Desktop/creator-pipeline/research_notes/SCAIL%202%20best%20practice%20settings/our_graph_vs_template.md)). The deliberate departures are `object_indices "0"` with `sort_by "area"` (template: all objects, left to right), the SAM driving text "person" against the template's "human" (the fp16 default) or "girl, handkerchief" (Int8 sample), portrait 512x896 where the template's landscape sample uses 896x512, the High tier at 16 steps and cfg 3.5 against the template's 40 and 5, a one-sentence prompt against a descriptive paragraph, explicit mp4/h264 saving, a `Trim Tail` safeguard, a side-by-side compare branch, an optional Finish pass and the off-by-default grey cut-out chain. The template has nothing we lack that affects pixels: its switches, math nodes and segment estimate are editor conveniences we compute in Python, and its mask previews are disabled. Two of our departures are improvements the template would benefit from (the identity filter, the compare branch), one is neutral (container), and three are the things to change: the High preset, the prompt, and the cut-out colour and edge.

## Conclusion

The research changes the picture from "our High tier is too weak" to "our inputs were wrong and our High tier is unpublished". The official Comfy example quality comes from the turbo path we already copy, run on a clean, single-person, well-framed reference with a long prompt; the difference between the example and our two live runs is almost entirely in the reference photo, the identity filter and the LoRA family, all of which are free to change and author-backed. The sampler question is real but smaller than it looked, and it has an uncomfortable shape: the only author-grade non-distilled preset costs about 13x a Standard render, so the tier the team will actually iterate on has to be designed by us from the maintainers' direction rather than copied from anyone.

Two implications for the roadmap. First, the app should treat reference preparation as a first-class step with its own checks (aspect, full body, single person, same SAM noun on both tracks, mask preview), because every documented failure mode for replacement mode is an input failure the model cannot recover from. Second, the pod session should be run as a measurement session as much as a quality session: eight single-segment renders at about $0.10 to $1 each will turn the project's SCAIL-2 cost figures from inferred to measured, and will tell the team whether the quality dial is resolution (704p turbo), steps (Plus), or both.
